Use case: logo-brand / stylized-concept.
Asset type: a proposed competitive ranked emblem for UMBRAL, an original first-person retro boomer shooter set in a dark fantasy Mictlan, with obsidian architecture and mystical weapons.
Style: premium original hand-painted PIXEL ART game badge, not a vector icon, not a photorealistic render. Deliberate visible square pixels and small clusters, crisp contour, compact chunky planes, restrained pixel dithering, approximately 192 x 192 pixel design logic enlarged with nearest-neighbor. Rich but disciplined material shading with large readable masses. A fierce ceremonial Mesoamerican-inspired graphic language: stepped forms, chiseled angular contours. Fictional original ornament, no claims of historical authenticity. Strong recognizable silhouette and readability as a small HUD emblem. Frontal orthographic presentation, near bilateral symmetry, no perspective tilt, fully visible with 15% safe empty margin on every edge. One isolated emblem centered on a square canvas, genuinely TRANSPARENT background. No text, letters, numerals, brand wordmarks, watermarks, mockup screens, extra small versions or variants. No wings, laurels, ribbons, generic medieval heraldic shield, esports animal heads, photorealism, hazy bloom, excessive tiny ornament, decorative scene or drop shadow background. Intended to share a coherent visual family with two other UMBRAL badges: gold ceremonial sun, silver skull, and black-violet fractured obsidian.
Subject: FIRST PLACE, SOL AZTECA, the most prestigious emblem. An imposing round ancient GOLD solar medallion, a stern abstract sun face at its center (strong rectangular brow, slit eyes, angular nose, no human portrait, no grinning skull), surrounded by a ring of eight broad angular stepped solar rays, with four cardinal rays slightly larger. Dark obsidian-black engraved channels split substantial gold planes. Tiny amber fire accents inside eye slits, elegant worn gold edges, deepest copper recesses and pale warm gold highlights. Gold dominates unmistakably. Substantial silhouette, controlled detail, visually majestic and intimidating without becoming busy. No literal reproduction of the Aztec Sun Stone; invent a unique game emblem.
